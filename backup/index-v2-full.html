FAQ

Good questions

Can't find what you need? Text us at (555) 010-SOHO — real humans, fast replies.

Do SohoPouch pouches contain nicotine or tobacco?
Never. Every SohoPouch is 100% nicotine-free and tobacco-free. The pouch is made from plant fibre and filled with functional ingredients and natural flavours.
How do I use a pouch?
Place one pouch between your upper lip and gum. Leave it in for 30–45 minutes — you'll feel it within about five — then dispose of it in the tin's back compartment or a bin.
How much caffeine is in a Caffeine pouch?
Each Caffeine pouch contains 100mg of natural caffeine (about one cup of coffee) paired with L-theanine. We recommend no more than 4 pouches per day and none within 6 hours of bedtime.
Can I combine different ranges in one day?
Yes — that's the idea. Caffeine in the morning, Electrolyte around training, Sleep in the evening. Just don't use Caffeine and Sleep together.
Is the Sleep pouch habit-forming?
Sleep pouches are melatonin-free and use magnesium, L-theanine and chamomile — ingredients that support relaxation without dependence. Talk to your doctor if you take sleep medication.
How does the subscription work?
Choose your tins and a delivery rhythm (2, 4 or 6 weeks). You save 20% on every order with free shipping. Skip, swap, pause or cancel anytime from your account or by SMS — no fees.
Where do you ship?
We ship to all 50 US states. Orders over $35 ship free and usually arrive in 2–5 business days.
What if I don't like it?
Your first order is covered by our 30-day guarantee. If it's not for you, let us know and we'll refund you — no need to send anything back.
